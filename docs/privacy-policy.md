# Tabmate Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 21 October 2026*

Tabmate is an app for tracking and splitting shared expenses with friends, flatmates and groups. This policy explains what data Tabmate collects, why it collects it and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| **Name and email address** | To create your account and show you to the friends you split with. |
| **Mobile number** (optional) | So friends can add you by number and find you in their contacts. |
| **Expenses, payments, groups, comments and notes** you or your friends add | This is the core of the app: working out who owes whom. |
| **UPI ID / PayPal / Venmo handle** (optional) | Only shown to friends, to fill in a payment link when they pay you back. Tabmate never moves money. |
| **Push notification token** | To send you notifications about expenses, payments and reminders. |
| **Contacts** (only if you tap "Find friends from contacts" and allow it) | The phone numbers and emails in your contacts are sent to our server **only** to check which contacts already use Tabmate. They are not stored and not used for anything else. |

We don't collect your location, don't show ads and don't sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

## Who can see your data

- **Friends and group members** see your name, the expenses and payments you share with them, and your payment handles if you added them.
- **Our service providers** store and process data on our behalf:
  - Supabase stores the database and handles sign-in.
  - Expo delivers push notifications.
  - Google/Firebase is used for Android push delivery.

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## People who aren't on Tabmate yet

If you add someone by email or mobile number who hasn't joined, Tabmate stores the name you give them and that email or number, so the shared expenses are waiting for them when they sign up. They can ask us to remove this at any time.

## Your choices

- **Edit:** you can change your name, mobile number and payment handles at any time under Account.
- **Notifications:** turn them off under Account → Notifications.
- **Delete your account:** go to Account → Security & app lock → **Delete my account** in the app. If you can't use the app, use the [account deletion request](delete-account.md).

  Deleting your account permanently removes your login, name, email, mobile number, payment handles, notifications and friend connections. Expenses you shared with others stay in their history under the name "Deleted user", so their balances remain correct.

## Children

Tabmate is not intended for children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
