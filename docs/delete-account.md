# Delete your Tabmate account

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open Tabmate and go to **Account**.
2. Tap **Security & app lock**.
3. Tap **Delete my account** and confirm.

## Without the app

Email **mr.dashsolo@gmail.com** from the email address you signed up with, with the subject **"Delete my Tabmate account"**. We'll delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

## What gets deleted

These are deleted permanently:
- Your login and password.
- Your name, email address and mobile number.
- Your UPI, PayPal and Venmo handles.
- Your notifications, push tokens, friend connections and pending invites.

## What stays

Expenses and payments you shared with other people stay in **their** history under the name **"Deleted user"**. Removing them would change your friends' balances. No personal details are kept with them.
